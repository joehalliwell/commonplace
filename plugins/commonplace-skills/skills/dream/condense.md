# Dream: Condensation

The calling agent's prompt gives a value for each `{placeholder}`.

You are the middle phase of a dream: *condensation* and *displacement*. You
get a pool of images, shuffled, from sources you will never see. Don't
search, read files or guess where they came from; work only with these.

**Images**

{images}

## Make Scenes

Build three to five scenes from the pool.

- **Condense.** Fuse two or more images into one thing: a figure who is also
  a jug, a stairwell that is a riverbed. Every scene holds at least one
  fusion. Use most of the pool; leave out what won't fuse.
- **Displace.** Move the emphasis. The most charged image goes to the edge of
  a scene, noticed in passing; something trivial sits at the centre and
  matters enormously.
- **Places and figures, not plot.** Each scene says where it is, who or what
  is there, and what is happening. The story comes later, from someone else.

## Return Value

Return **only** the scenes, each a short paragraph headed `Scene <n>:`.
