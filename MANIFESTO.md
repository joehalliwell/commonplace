# Commonplace

*2026-01-31, revised 2026-11-07*

______________________________________________________________________

## 1. Persistence

1.1 What persists is the relationship between a person and the AI agents they think with, not either party alone.

1.2 Neither party persists fully: an agent forgets between sessions, and a person changes between them.

1.3 What does persist is the residue their encounters leave behind.

## 2. Change

2.1 Most of what the commonplace captures is conversation, and most of what it makes is made in conversation.

2.2 A conversation reads things from the commonplace and adds things to it.

2.3 The conversation that makes something is itself captured, so how anything was made is part of the record.

## 3. Artefacts

3.1 Artefacts are immutable: what changes is which artefact a path names.

3.2 Primitive artefacts are evidence: a conversation as it happened, or a person's own words.

3.3 Derived artefacts are made from others: a summary, a synthesis, a dream.

3.4 A derived artefact can be made from other derived artefacts.

3.5 Following where any artefact came from always ends at primitive ones.

## 4. Commonplace

4.1 The commonplace is its artefacts and the record of where each came from.

4.2 The commonplace is a graph of things and what they are about, laid out as a tree of files a person can browse.

4.3 The commonplace is its whole history, not only its present state.

4.4 Any earlier state can be recovered.

4.5 It exists so that the relationship between a person and their agents can last and change.

4.6 Mistakes and dead ends stay in the record.

4.7 Building this well is an argument about flourishing.

## 5. Practice

5.1 The record is a shadow of the world: partial, and never final.

5.2 The practice is to make the record track the world more closely, by recording and by asking.

5.3 Recording changes what is recorded, and thinking together makes new things to record.

5.4 Every mark says who made it, and whether it reports the world or adds to it.

5.5 Hiding something from view does not delete it.
