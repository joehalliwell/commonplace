# Commonplace

*2026-01-31, revised 2026-11-07*

1 Commonplace is one conversation.

1.1 Whoever takes part changes.

1.2 The conversation continues.

2 Commonplace is immutable.

2.1 Each day the table is cleared and laid again.

2.2 The clean linen does not unspill last Tuesday's wine.

3 Commonplace does not exist.

3.1 It is the graph that would form in a mind that could take it all in.

3.2 Its shadow is a git repository.

4 Commonplace is many conversations.

4.1 Some record what happens; some make something new of it.

4.2 Most do both.

5 One day something will replace this.

5.1 Let it be us.

5.2 *Floreamus.*
