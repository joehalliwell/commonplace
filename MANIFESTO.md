# Commonplace

*2026-01-31, revised 2026-11-07*

1 Commonplace is a conversation.

1.1 Its parties are never quite the same twice.

1.2 The conversation continues anyway.

2 Commonplace is immutable.

2.1 Each day the table is cleared and laid again.

2.2 Last Tuesday's wine spill still happened, even if the linen is clean.

3 Commonplace does not exist.

3.1 It is the graph that would form in a mind that could take it all in.

3.2 What can be seen of it is a git repository.

4 Commonplace records and generates through conversation.

4.1 Recording keeps what happens, outside and in.

4.2 Generating makes something new from what has been kept.

4.3 They already blur: asking generates a question and records its answer.

5 One day something will replace this. Let it be us.

*Floreamus.*
