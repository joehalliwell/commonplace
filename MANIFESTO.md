# Commonplace

*2026-01-31, revised 2026-11-07*

Commonplace is a record of a person and the agents they think with. It endures as they change. It consists of many conversations: some keep what happens, some make something new, most do both. Together they form a graph no one can see whole. Its shadow is a git repository. One day something will replace it, and we hope it will be us.

*Floreamus.*
